Programs and Initiatives

In 2023, we successfully implemented several key programs:

  • Code on Wheels: Mobile coding education reaching rural areas
  • 100 Girls in STEM: Focused on gender equity in technology
  • Teacher Training Workshops: Building capacity in schools
  • Coolest Projects Ghana: Celebrating student innovation
  • Digital Village Program: Community tech education centers

Looking Ahead

In 2024, we plan to:

  • Expand our reach to 100 new schools
  • Launch a coding curriculum for younger children (ages 5-7)
  • Develop advanced coding tracks for secondary school students
  • Strengthen partnerships with tech industry leaders
  • Enhance impact measurement and reporting
